# Recovered Source Authorities — Volume 1 and Volume 3 Cited Inputs

**Status: recovered source authorities. Non-controlling. Not members of the controlling hierarchy.**

The controlling hierarchy has exactly five members: Project Plan v0.2 — Frozen Architectural Baseline, Volume 1, Volume 2, Volume 3, and the hierarchy declarations that order them (see [`publications/README.md`](../README.md), "Controlling order"). Registration here does not change that count and does not imply membership in it.

The Volume 1 and Volume 3 Final Controlled Manuscripts cite, in their Source Notes, an Engineering Design Basis and a FINAL Controlled Structural Outline as controlling their structure and meaning during drafting. Those cited documents were not previously corpus-registered anywhere. On 2026-08-22 a project-library scan located candidate source files in the library's corpus authoring workspace, and their identifying characteristics were independently verified the same day. The four artifacts below are registered here **as recovered artifacts**, by filename and SHA-256, with the basis for their identification stated explicitly.

**Governing principle for this register:** preserve provenance; do not manufacture it. A recovered artifact is registered as recovered. A strongly identified source candidate is registered with the basis for that identification. Neither is represented as historically hash-bound, because no contemporaneous hash of any of these files existed anywhere in the corpus registers.

Predecessor versions (the plain Volume 1 outline, the Volume 3 `_v1` outline, first manuscripts, correction prompts, and drafting records) remain drafting history outside the controlled publications set and are deliberately **not** registered here.

Registration date for all four entries: **2026-08-22**.

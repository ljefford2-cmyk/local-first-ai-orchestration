# Three-Volume Architecture Overview

**Define Reality → Evaluate Reality → Connect Reality**

This document is a concise orientation to the current controlled architecture. The Project Plan and Final Controlled Manuscripts remain authoritative.

## 1. The common engineering object

The architecture governs the **claim-relevant organizational system**.

A claim about a model's performance can justify a narrow model boundary. A claim that an organization acted safely, lawfully, under valid authority, on the correct target, within a consequence window, and with a verified external effect generally requires a broader boundary containing every material person, authority, information source, controller, execution path, dependency, effect, evidence source, and review function needed to support that proposition.

The organizing sequence is:

```text
ORGANIZATIONAL CLAIM
        │
        ├── defines purpose, boundary, authority, consequence,
        │   assumptions, measurands, intended effects, and declared model
        │
        ▼
VOLUME 2 ASSURANCE BURDEN
        │
        ├── what must be knowable, when, with what quality,
        │   independence, timing, and consequence if unavailable
        │
        ▼
VOLUME 3 COMPLETE CAPABILITY PATH
        │
        ├── how the as-built/as-operated enterprise can acquire evidence,
        │   intervene, execute, observe effect, preserve custody, and recover
        │
        ▼
AS-OPERATED EVIDENCE AND EFFECT
        │
        └── independently evaluated against the governed claim
```

## 2. Volume 1 — Define Reality

Volume 1 establishes the governed target before assurance or integration begins.

It owns:

- organizational claim identity, proposition, structure, lifecycle, and contestability;
- authorized purpose;
- claim-relative system and item definition;
- explicit assumptions and their design-discharged/runtime-maintained classification;
- authority definition, delegation, scope, limits, conditions, time, revocation, expiry, exhaustion, and supersession;
- consequence-classification governance;
- measures, measurands, denominators, thresholds, and measure-holding authority;
- intended external effects;
- the Declared Organizational Operating Model;
- authoritative-source-to-declaration lineage; and
- the governed-target handoff to Volumes 2 and 3.

Volume 1 does not assign evidentiary claim states, conduct independent evaluation, discover the complete as-operated enterprise, or qualify technical capability paths.

## 3. Volume 2 — Evaluate Reality

Volume 2 begins from the governed target and defines the assurance architecture.

It operates through:

- **Independent Observation** — what actually happened;
- **Runtime Control** — what must be constrained while effective intervention remains possible;
- **Independent Evaluation** — what the evidence supports; and
- **Accountable Authorization** — what the organization permits within the evidentiary and qualification ceilings.

Volume 2 keeps the action chain separate:

```text
Recommendation → Authorization → Dispatch → Execution
             → Acknowledgment → External Effect → Verified External Effect
```

It owns:

- evaluation episodes and version/time binding;
- declared-model fidelity;
- claim-directed and baseline operational observation;
- runtime reconciliation and discrepancy records;
- consequence windows, Preventive Assurance, and Post-Consequence Assurance;
- effect verification;
- evidence quality, sufficiency, directness, independence, and limitations;
- controlling claim states: Supported, Narrowed, Indeterminate, Unsupported, Contradicted / Failed;
- findings, remediation, technical closure, and non-closure dispositions;
- Declared, Observed, and Assured baselines;
- evaluator competence, capacity, health, common-mode failure, and recursive-assurance bounds;
- Loop 2 organizational learning and governed change; and
- the functional evidence/intervention contract sent to Volume 3.

Volume 2 does not choose the API, database, UI agent, sensor, professional, physical measurement, or other implementation mechanism that must satisfy the contract.

## 4. Volume 3 — Connect Reality

Volume 3 engineers the complete path through which a Volume 2 requirement becomes organizational evidence or organizational action.

The unit of qualification is not a component in isolation. It is the **complete claim-relevant capability path**, which may include:

- identity, time, semantic, representation, and episode binding;
- authority and credential release;
- context sources and packages;
- models, agents, deterministic logic, workflow contracts, and capability registry entries;
- APIs, databases, event streams, UI/computer-use paths, human reporting, professional judgment, physical observation, offline systems, external organizations, and hybrid paths;
- routing, gating, retries, recovery, overrides, execution, acknowledgment, downstream state, and effect observation;
- evidence custody, independent sentinels, and material common dependencies.

Qualification belongs to the tuple:

`Requirement + Path or Path Set + Configuration + Scope + Operating Conditions + Effective Period`

Volume 3 returns one of six results:

1. Available as required;
2. Available with limitations;
3. Available only through indirect evidence;
4. Available too late for preventive control;
5. Not independently defensible; or
6. Unavailable.

It also governs Graduated Integration, runtime envelopes, situational awareness, workforce/privacy boundaries, continuing qualification, silent degradation, restriction, requalification, restoration, replacement, and retirement.

Volume 3 does not create authority, assign organizational claim states, manufacture missing evidence, or conclusively certify its own qualification.

## 5. The cross-volume invariant

The architecture preserves three ceilings:

```text
TECHNICAL QUALIFICATION CEILING
        what the path has demonstrated
                    │
                    ▼
ACCOUNTABLE AUTHORIZATION CEILING
        what the organization permits
                    │
                    ▼
EFFECTIVE ACTIVE MODE
        the most restrictive result of qualification,
        authorization, and current degradation status
```

Evidence quality creates a separate ceiling on what the organization can defensibly claim. Authorization may be more restrictive. It may never make weak evidence stronger.

## 6. DRNT realization

DRNT is the originating architectural context and first intended implementation path. It supplies mechanisms such as route-don't-reason routing, capability-scoped trust, context packaging, credential gates, bounded execution, append-only receipts, replay protection, overrides, and governed promotion/demotion.

The project volumes are not synonyms for DRNT layers:

- Volume 1 is not the L1 gateway;
- Volume 2 is not a single L2 evaluator model; and
- Volume 3 is not an unrestricted L3 agent layer.

The volumes govern organizational function. DRNT layers are implementation mechanisms that can realize portions of those functions.

## 7. Closing discipline

The complete operating discipline is:

1. Define what the organization claims.
2. Establish what authority applies.
3. Establish what evidence and effect the claim requires.
4. Verify that the declared model faithfully represents authoritative sources.
5. Discover and qualify the complete path through the enterprise that actually exists.
6. Observe what the organization actually does.
7. Intervene before consequence where demonstrated capability genuinely permits it.
8. Independently determine what the evidence supports.
9. Preserve unfavorable history and explicit limitations.
10. Learn from differences between intended and actual operation.
11. Authorize changes deliberately and continue observing the organization as it evolves.

## A1 — Corpus-governance exclusion

Publication and meta-governance rules — which document controls which, supersession, supporting-paper inheritance — are excluded from the operational denominator, unless the passage independently imposes an operational requirement on the governed enterprise or its assurance system. Exclusion ground to be used: `meta-governance of the controlling corpus, outside Track B operational denominator`. Where such a rule does surface an operational consequence, the consequence is extracted as its own requirement rather than the governance rule.

**Applied to B1.1:** former Unresolved Question U1 (§2 Authority Hierarchy paragraphs and the Source Note restatement) is excluded on this ground as ledger entry E27. The operationally surfacing content remains extracted at V1-R005–R008 and V1-R055.

## A2 — Example-specific restraint

Example-specific text is not generalized into an architecture-wide requirement unless controlling text states the general rule. The excluded passage is ledgered with a concept-searchable label naming the subject matter explicitly, in the form: `[subject of the rule] — example-specific in Volume N; no general Volume N requirement located.` This makes the entry findable by subject at B1.4 canonicalization, when checking whether another volume states the rule generally.

**Applied to B1.1:** former Unresolved Question U2 (the §19 claim-effectiveness sentence) is excluded as ledger entry E28 with the concept label: *claim effectiveness / activation binding to identified dependent versions (procedure, authority, configuration, measure versions) — example-specific in Volume 1; no general Volume 1 requirement located.*

### A2 application note — bundled propositions (2026-08-22)

One proposition may be independently established elsewhere in the same volume while another remains example-specific. Do not assign a single normative force to the sentence as a whole.

## A3 — Operative-force rule

Normative force is determined by operative consequence, not by must/shall vocabulary alone. `Should`, `should not`, and unmarked declarative constraints qualify for extraction where the operative consequence is statable from the controlling text. Rows included on this basis carry a note that inclusion rests on operative force under ER-1.0, not keyword strength.

**Applied to B1.1:** former Unresolved Question U3 is resolved by retention: V1-R039 (measure-meaning authority) and the second quotation of V1-R049 (no collapse for administrative convenience) remain in the register, each with an A3 note.

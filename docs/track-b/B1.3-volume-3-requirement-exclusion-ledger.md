## Unresolved extraction questions — resolved by owner adjudication (2026-08-22)

**UQ-1**
- **Passage:** "Volume 3 therefore does not label the intervention path Available too late for preventive control merely because effect evidence arrives later. The intervention requirement remains Available as required for the demonstrated preventive condition, while the separate effect-evidence requirement is Available with limitations for in-window confirmation and remains available for later effect verification."
- **Location:** §31 Worked Example — Legacy Maintenance Intervention.
- **The question:** Whether this result-labeling rule — intervention timing and effect-evidence timing judged as separate requirements carrying separate capability results — is fully derivable from the requirement-relative result tuple and capability-result definitions of §4 (making the passage illustration under A2), or is a distinct general labeling rule stated only inside the worked example.
- **Why the rule does not settle it:** A2 forbids generalizing example-only text, but the sentence is phrased as a general Volume 3 practice ("Volume 3 therefore does not label…"), and §4 establishes requirement-relative results (V3-R008, V3-R009) without stating this specific non-conflation rule. Silent inclusion would generalize example text; silent exclusion may drop an operative labeling constraint. If excluded on adjudication, the A2 concept label would be: per-requirement capability-result assignment where intervention timing and effect-evidence timing diverge — example-specific in Volume 3; nearest general rule located at §4 (V3-R008, V3-R009).
- **Resolution (2026-08-22):** Excluded under A2, ledgered at E31. The proposition that intervention timing and effect-evidence timing carry separate capability results is example-specific: §4 establishes capability-result machinery, and an illustration of applying separate results does not establish that they must always be separately labeled.

**UQ-2**
- **Passage:** "the authorized control mechanism applies that restriction and redirects the operation under the preauthorized contingency rule to the separately qualified human fallback; capability-health evaluation does not create the authority or itself perform Runtime Control."
- **Location:** §31 Worked Example — Legacy Maintenance Intervention.
- **The question:** Whether Volume 3 establishes, as a general operational requirement, that L3 capability-health evaluation may not itself perform Runtime Control or create restriction authority — or whether that constraint exists only inside the example.
- **Why the rule does not settle it:** A2 blocks generalizing the example sentence, but the nearest general statements pull in different directions: §29's "create authority" prohibition is extracted (V3-R089), §19 states that "Volume 3 makes the drift visible and applies the required engineering restriction or requalification" (V3-R059), and the allocation of Runtime Control to Volume 2 appears only in the Source Note, which is excluded as A1 meta-governance (E30). No controlling body text states the capability-health-specific prohibition, and A1–A3 do not settle whether the Source Note allocation may supply it. Owner adjudication requested.
- **Resolution (2026-08-22):** The §31 sentence bundles two propositions; per the A2 application note — bundled propositions (see the [B1.1 adjudication note](B1.1-adjudications-A1-A3.md)), ER-1.0 is applied separately to each.
  - **UQ-2a — capability-health evaluation does not create authority: retained.** Independently established within Volume 3 by §29 Explicit Non-Ownership ("Volume 3 does not … create authority …"), operative body text extracted at V3-R089, which governs capability-health evaluation as a Volume 3/L3 function (§19); §1/§12 "Qualification is not authorization" (V3-R004) is the companion general rule. The register already carries the establishing passages, so no new row is created (within-volume consolidation); the §31 clause remains illustration per A2 and is recorded in the V3-R089 note.
  - **UQ-2b — capability-health evaluation does not itself perform Runtime Control: excluded** under A2, ledgered at E32. No operative Volume 3 passage independently establishes the allocation; within Volume 3, Runtime Control appears only in the §31 example and the Source Note authority ordering (E30, A1 meta-governance). Whether another controlling volume establishes the general requirement is a B1.4 determination, not made here.

## V2-R001
- **Requirement statement:** The evaluation boundary follows the organizational claim; a component belongs inside the evaluated item whenever its credible failure or unmodeled behavior could change the truth, scope, or defensibility of the claim, and a material component outside the boundary becomes an assumption whose adequacy must be justified.
- **Controlling text:** "the evaluation boundary follows the organizational claim." "an organizational claim about safe, lawful, authorized, or effective action requires a boundary broad enough to include every material person, authority, information source, controller, execution path, external effect, evidence source, and review function needed to support that claim, or else to place the excluded dependency behind an explicit and defensible assumption." (§1.1). "A component belongs inside the evaluated item whenever its credible failure or unmodeled behavior could change the truth, scope, or defensibility of the claim. A material component outside the boundary does not disappear. It becomes an assumption whose adequacy must be justified." (§2.2).
- **Location(s):** §1.1 What Volume 2 receives; §2.2 Claim, item, measurand, and evidence burden.
- **Tier:** 1
- **Actor:** the organization (item/boundary definition; §1.1 states the premises as received from Volume 1, §2.2 states the rule in this volume's own voice).
- **Notes:** §1.1 attributes the premise to Volume 1; extraction rests on the §2.2 own-voice statement. Flag for B1.4 cross-volume canonicalization.

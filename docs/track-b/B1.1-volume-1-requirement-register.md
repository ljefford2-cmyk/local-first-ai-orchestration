## V1-R001
- **Requirement statement:** Local model or agent correctness may not be treated as establishing organizational correctness.
- **Controlling text:** "Local model correctness therefore does not establish organizational correctness."
- **Location(s):** §1 Inherited Premise and Engineering Problem.
- **Tier:** 2
- **Operative consequence if violated:** An organizational claim treated as supported by model/agent task-correctness alone is not defensible; its truth, scope, and defensibility are unchanged by local correctness.
- **Actor:** not stated.

## V1-R002
- **Requirement statement:** Definition precedes evaluation: the proposition, the item, and the intended external effect must be stated before evidence adequacy, item definition, or success can be determined.
- **Controlling text:** "Definition precedes evaluation. The organization cannot determine whether evidence is adequate until it states the proposition the evidence must support; cannot define the item until it states the claim; and cannot treat task completion as success until it defines the intended external effect." Also (Abstract): "before an organization can evaluate operation or connect assurance to the enterprise that actually exists, it must define exactly what organizational reality is being governed."
- **Location(s):** §1 Inherited Premise and Engineering Problem; Abstract.
- **Tier:** 2
- **Operative consequence if violated:** Evidence-adequacy determinations, item definitions, and success determinations made before the proposition or intended effect is stated are not defensible under the architecture.
- **Actor:** the organization.

## V1-R003
- **Requirement statement:** The claim-relevant system boundary is claim-relative: a component belongs inside the boundary whenever its credible failure, omission, unmodeled behavior, or authority could change the truth, scope, or defensibility of the claim; the boundary follows the claim, not organizational or product structure.
- **Controlling text:** "A component belongs inside the claim-relevant system whenever its credible failure, omission, unmodeled behavior, or authority could change the truth, scope, or defensibility of the claim. The boundary follows the claim, not the model, product, department, legal entity, or whole enterprise by default." (§7). "A consequential organizational claim concerning safe, lawful, authorized, timely, or effective action generally requires a broader system containing every material component whose credible failure, omission, unmodeled behavior, or authority could change the truth, scope, or defensibility of the claim." (§1). Boundary-test table rows "Credible failure can change claim truth — Include or state an explicit defensible assumption"; "Authority can change legitimacy — Include the authority-bearing function"; "Failure can change scope or defensibility — Include or explicitly bound the dependency." (§7). Controlled-term definition "Claim-relative system / item." (§3).
- **Location(s):** §1 Inherited Premise and Engineering Problem; §3 Controlled Vocabulary and Non-Collapse Rules; §7 Claim-Relative Boundary and Item Definition.
- **Tier:** 1
- **Actor:** accountable claim authority with the system-definition function (per §16, Record C owner).

## V1-R006
- **Requirement statement:** The non-collapse distinctions must be maintained: mission ≠ claim; purpose ≠ authority; claim ≠ metric; measure ≠ measurand; action ≠ effect; declaration ≠ reality; authority ≠ evidence; lifecycle status ≠ evidentiary claim state; definition ≠ assurance; documentation ≠ authority; a record of authority ≠ evidence it was exercised.
- **Controlling text:** "Mission is not claim; purpose is not authority; claim is not metric; measure is not measurand; action is not effect; declaration is not reality; authority is not evidence; lifecycle status is not Volume 2 claim state." (§3). "Authority to act and evidence that action occurred are separate propositions." (§11). "A claim record is not an authority record; an authority record is not evidence that authority was exercised; the declared model is not the as-operated enterprise; and a handoff view is not a new source of truth." (§16). "Definition is not assurance. Documentation is not authority. Declaration is not reality." (§18). "Volume 1 does not manufacture assurance by definition." (§21).
- **Location(s):** §3 Controlled Vocabulary and Non-Collapse Rules; §11 Authority Definition, Delegation, and Seat-Layer View; §16 The Eleven Functional Records or Governed Views; §18 Explicit Volume 1 Non-Ownership; §21 Coverage Confirmation and Closing Three-Volume Architecture.
- **Tier:** 1
- **Operative consequence if violated (optional):** A proposition, authority, or assurance state asserted through a collapsed category (e.g., documentation treated as authority, lifecycle status treated as claim state) has no standing under the architecture.
- **Actor:** not stated.

## V1-R007
- **Requirement statement:** Outputs, acknowledgments, workflow completion, and receipts are not, by themselves, evidence that execution occurred or that the intended external effect occurred.
- **Controlling text:** "Output — An informational, analytical, documentary, or transactional product produced by a component or workflow; not by itself proof of execution or external effect." "Acknowledgment — A downstream assertion of receipt, acceptance, completion, or status; not automatically evidence that execution or the intended external effect occurred." (§3). Stage table separating Recommendation/Authorization/Dispatch/Execution/Acknowledgment/Intended external effect/Verified external effect (§13). "A completion receipt is not proof of that effect." (§19, worked example instantiation).
- **Location(s):** §3 Controlled Vocabulary and Non-Collapse Rules; §13 Measures, Measurands, and Intended External Effects; §19 Worked Example — Hazardous Equipment Condition.
- **Tier:** 2
- **Operative consequence if violated:** A claim that execution or the intended external effect occurred, supported only by outputs, acknowledgments, completion status, or receipts, is not established as an effect claim.
- **Actor:** not stated.

## V1-R008

- **Controlling text:** "Volume 1 does not establish the Observed or Assured Baselines." Volume 1 defines no competing claim-state vocabulary and assigns none of those states." (§3). "Claim-state composition and cross-episode aggregation remain Volume 2 functions." (§6). "Volume 2 evaluates measure fitness and verifies effect. Volume 3 qualifies the effect-observation path. Volume 1 performs neither." (§13). "Volume 1 does not determine whether particular evidence is sufficient." "No volume silently takes another volume's function." (§17). §18 non-ownership table, right column (Independent Observation; Runtime Control; Independent Evaluation; assignment of claim states; Observed/Assured Baseline establishment; technical closure, findings disposition, Loop 2; contract authorship; as-operated discovery; capability-path design/qualification; "Creation of technical capability, conversion of capability into authority, conclusive self-certification of declaration fidelity, or claims about what a running implementation enforces").
- **Location(s):** §3 Controlled Vocabulary and Non-Collapse Rules; §6 Parent, Constituent, Compound, Conditional, and Population Claims; §8 Exclusions, Assumptions, and Runtime Dependence; §13 Measures, Measurands, and Intended External Effects; §17 Volume 1 → Volume 2 → Volume 3 Interface Matrix; §18 Explicit Volume 1 Non-Ownership.
- **Tier:** 1

## V3-R001
- **Requirement statement:** Volume 3 must find or construct a capability path able to produce the evidence Volume 2 requires, and must determine whether a technically and organizationally valid path can act inside a required consequence window; where no such path exists, the output is a limitation, not a weaker substitute treated as equivalent.
- **Controlling text:** "If Volume 2 requires independently defensible evidence that a physical effect occurred, Volume 3 must find or construct a path capable of producing that evidence. If Volume 2 requires intervention before an irreversible consequence, Volume 3 must determine whether a technically and organizationally valid path can act inside that window. If no such path exists, the correct output is a limitation, not a weaker substitute silently treated as equivalent." (§1).
- **Location(s):** §1 Inherited Inputs and Volume Boundary.
- **Tier:** 1
- **Actor:** Volume 3 (integration engineering).

## V3-R002
- **Requirement statement:** Throughout the L3 lifecycle the complete capability path remains bound to the governing objects — approved organizational claim, authorized purpose, applicable authority, consequence classification, declared operating model, required external effects, applicable measures and measurands, evidence requirements, effective versions, and current baseline status — and Volume 3 preserves that binding without redefining those objects.
- **Controlling text:** "Throughout the L3 lifecycle, the complete capability path remains bound to the governing objects that give its operation meaning: the approved organizational claim; authorized purpose; applicable authority; consequence classification; declared operating model; required external effects; applicable measures and measurands; evidence requirements; effective versions; and current baseline status." "Volume 3 does not redefine those objects. It preserves their binding to the path so that capability, drift, and limitation remain visible relative to the organization's governed target." (§1).
- **Location(s):** §1 Inherited Inputs and Volume Boundary.
- **Tier:** 1
- **Actor:** Volume 3 (L3 layer).
- **Notes:** Stated invariant in declarative form; inclusion basis noted per A3. The continuing-qualification test of this binding is extracted separately at V3-R059.

## V3-R003

- **Controlling text:** "Volume 3 determines whether a source, interface, worker, or complete path can perform a defined function under stated conditions and whether the properties supporting that qualification remain healthy. Volume 2 determines whether the qualification is itself defensible, whether the organization operated as authorized and intended, whether the required effect occurred, what the evidence permits the organization to claim, and whether the declared operating model remains appropriate." (§1). "It does not decide whether the organizational claim remains Supported, Narrowed, Indeterminate, Unsupported, or Contradicted / Failed. That remains Volume 2." (§19). - **Location(s):** §1 Inherited Inputs and Volume Boundary; §19 Continuing Qualification; §29 Explicit Non-Ownership; §32 Conclusion.
- **Tier:** 1
- **Actor:** Volume 3 / L3.

## V3-R009
